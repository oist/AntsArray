03 · Why two activity groups?

Seven speed measurements × three quantiles across hourly estimates give 21 features. After robust scaling and three activity PCs, KMeans with K=2 has the highest silhouette in both colonies and passes the group-size, ant-bootstrap and A/B replication criteria. Spatial labels and day 2 do not choose K.

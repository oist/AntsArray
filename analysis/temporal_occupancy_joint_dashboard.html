What do the groups mean?

Behavior summaries describe the groups after clustering; they were not clustering inputs. Ants can contribute to multiple groups. Bootstrap agreement measures reproducibility when an ant is left out, rather than the probability of a biological task.

The selected map and the group's typical map share one color scale. The typical map averages all assigned windows with weights proportional to observed hours.
